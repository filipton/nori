nori is a music player for your own Navidrome, Subsonic or OpenSubsonic server, and for octo-fiesta. One
Rust core does the playing, the queue, the lyrics and the settings; each app around it is a thin shell
for its platform. These pages apply to nori 0.6.

- **New here?** Start with [What nori is](start/what-is-nori.md), then [Install](start/install.md) and
  [Connect a server](start/connect-a-server.md).
- **Looking for a setting?** The [settings reference](https://norifm.com/docs/settings/) lists every one, with its default.
- **Wondering what leaves your device?** See [Privacy and network](privacy.md).
- **Building on it?** The [developer pages](developers/architecture.md) cover the core and the clients.
