Applies to nori 0.6.

**Which servers does nori work with?** Navidrome, any Subsonic or OpenSubsonic server, and octo-fiesta.
It does not play from Jellyfin, Plex or local files on the phone.

**Is there an app for iPhone?** No. The Apple client runs on the iPod touch 6th generation on iOS 12,
jailbroken.

**Does nori cast to Chromecast or UPnP speakers?** No. On Android, the output button opens the system's
output switcher, and [remote control](../connected/remote-control.md) moves the music between your own
devices with nori.

**Does nori need an account?** Only the one on your own server. nori has no accounts and no analytics.
See [Privacy and network](../privacy.md).

**Can I back up my settings?** Not yet.

**Where do I get it?** From the [releases on GitHub](https://github.com/norifm/nori/releases). See
[Install](../start/install.md).
