Applies to nori 0.6. "All" means Android, the desktop, the terminal and the iPod touch.

## Servers

- Navidrome, Subsonic, OpenSubsonic and octo-fiesta; token, API-key and legacy sign-in. All.
- A second address for away from home, several servers to switch between. All.
- Extra HTTP headers, self-signed and client certificates, Wi-Fi only per server. Android.

## Library

- Albums, artists, songs, playlists, genres, favourites, years, folders, internet radio. All.
- Instant search from an index on the device, merged with the server's results. All.
- Playlists, smart playlists from rules, M3U import and export. Playlists all; the smart playlist editor
  on Android.
- Listening history, and listening stats for any period. Android.
- Mixes from a taste model that learns on the device. Android, the desktop and the iPod.

## Playback

- Gapless, crossfade, AutoMix with beat matching. All.
- Speed and pitch: Android, the desktop and the terminal. Skipping silence: all.
- ReplayGain by song, album or automatically, with a loudness target. All.
- A queue that survives restarts, shuffle that spreads artists apart, autofill when the queue ends. All.
- Sleep timer. Android and the iPod.
- Offload to the audio chip, high quality output. Android.

## Sound

- Graphic and parametric equalizer, presets, compressor, noise gate, loudness compensation, crossfeed,
  balance, mono, limiter. All, with the effects on Android, the desktop and the terminal.
- AutoEQ curves for 8,850 headphones. All.
- A sound for each output. All.

## Lyrics

- Your server's lyrics, then sixteen services online, scored and the best shown. All.
- Word-by-word lyrics, translations, timing you can nudge. All.

## Offline

- Downloads of songs, albums, playlists, artists or everything; a stream cache. All.
- Changes made offline sent later; playing on from downloads when the server drops. All.

## Connected

- Remote control between your devices. All.
- Jams with a link or QR code, with requests and listening along. All; needs octo-fiesta.

## Platform

- Widgets, Android Auto, the notification and lock screen. Android.
- Media keys and MPRIS. The desktop on Linux.
- Covers drawn in the terminal (kitty, sixel, iTerm2). The terminal.
- The lock screen and the headphone remote. The iPod.
