Applies to nori 0.6 on Android.

Long-press the home screen, choose Widgets and find nori. Five widgets:

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Player | what's playing, with its controls; resize it for a bigger cover |
| Cover | the cover of what's playing, with play and pause |
| For you | your favourites and today's mixes |
| Random albums | a few albums from your library, shuffled when you want |
| Lyrics | the line being sung, as it's sung |

The widgets update when something changes, not on a timer.
