Applies to nori 0.6 on Android.

nori shows in Android Auto with its own tabs: Home, Library, Favorites and Downloads, with your mixes,
artists and genres. A song picked from a list plays that list from there. Voice search finds songs and
plays by artist, album, playlist, genre or mix. Covers come through to the car's screen, and the
controls include repeat and radio.

In a car, the controls and tabs sit on the driver's side and the cover on the other; the controls' side
can be set to the driver's side, the left or the right.

If nori does not appear in the car, see [Install](../start/install.md#android): Android Auto hides apps
that were not installed from the Play Store until Unknown sources is on.
