---
title: Per-output sound
description: Giving the speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device and each USB DAC its own sound.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Applies to nori 0.6. Android and the desktop list every output; the iPod has its speaker, the headphone
jack and Bluetooth.

With **Remember sound per device** on (the default, in Settings, Sound), nori remembers the sound for each place the music
goes: the phone's speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device by name and each USB DAC. When the
output changes, its sound comes with it.

For each output you can choose:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| Flat | the equalizer off on this device |
| No processing | no equalizer or effects on this device; volume levelling and transitions still apply |
| Leave as is | nothing switches and nothing is offered |
| A saved profile | one of your equalizer profiles |
| An AutoEQ curve | the curve for that headphone, see [AutoEQ](autoeq.md) |

**Forget this device** removes an output and its sound from the list.
