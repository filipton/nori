Applies to nori 0.6 on Android. The other clients have the same pages in their own layout.

1. **Home** shows your shelves: recently played, newest and most played albums, top songs, random
   albums, favourites and the playlists you pinned. Which rows show, and in what order, is yours to
   choose in Settings.
2. **Library** lists albums, artists, songs, playlists, genres, favourites, years, folders, radio and
   downloads.
3. **Search** answers as you type, from an index kept on the phone, and adds the server's results when
   you pause. See [Library and search](../using/library-and-search.md).
4. **An album page** opens with its cover across the top, melting into a colour taken from it. Play and
   Shuffle sit under the title; each song has a menu, and a swipe on a row queues it or makes it a
   favourite.
5. **The player** opens from the bar at the bottom. Swipe the cover to skip, down to close. From there,
   open the lyrics, the queue, or the output button to choose where the music plays.
6. **Settings** has a search field: type what you are after, such as "gapless" or "lyrics".
