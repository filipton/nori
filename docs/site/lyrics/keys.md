title: Keys for lyrics services
description: The two lyrics services that work better, or at all, with your own key.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Applies to nori 0.6 on Android, the desktop and the terminal.

Two services take a key of your own, entered in Settings, Lyrics:

| Key | What it adds |
|---|---|
| PaxSenix key | PaxSenix's Musixmatch and Spotify lyrics. Without it, those two are skipped quietly. |
| BetterLyrics key | lyrics for songs BetterLyrics has not stored yet. Without one it answers only for songs it already has. |

Keys are kept with your settings on the device and sent only to their own service.
