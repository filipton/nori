Applies to nori 0.6. The display settings are Android's; the terminal's keys are listed below.

## Lyrics that drift

nori checks lyrics against the song's voice and shifts them when they run late. When they still drift,
**Source and timing**, the corner under the lyrics, says where they came from and nudges them sooner or
later. In the terminal, `[` and `]` move the words later or sooner, and `0` puts them back.

## Translations

**Show translations** (on by default) shows a translation under each line when your server has one.

## Display

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Fill in words as they're sung | on | for lyrics timed word by word |
| Text size | medium | small, medium or large |
| Keep the screen on | on | while lyrics are shown and music plays |

Tap a line to play from it.
